Frequently Asked Questions

How can I sponsor a child?

Visit our Sponsor a Child page, choose a child, and follow the donation process. You can also contact us directly.

Can I visit the orphanage?

Yes, we welcome visitors. Please arrange an appointment by emailing us at least two weeks in advance.

What are the volunteering opportunities?

We need teachers, medical volunteers, and activity coordinators. Send us a message with your skills and availability.

How are donations used?

100% of donations go to children's education, healthcare, food, and shelter. We publish annual reports for transparency.
